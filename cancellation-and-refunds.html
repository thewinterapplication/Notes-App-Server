Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated: March 26, 2026

1. Cancellation

If your plan supports recurring billing, you may request cancellation from inside the app or by contacting support. When cancellation is accepted at cycle end, your paid access continues until the end of the current billing period.

2. Refund Eligibility

  • Refunds are generally not provided for successfully delivered digital access that has already been activated.
  • If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, we will review the duplicate transaction.
  • If money was debited but the payment failed and access was not granted, we will review the payment status and guide you on reversal or refund processing.

3. Review Process

Refund and dispute requests are reviewed after checking Razorpay gateway records, payment IDs, subscription state, and backend activation logs.

4. How to Request Help

Email thewinterapplication@gmail.com with your registered phone number, payment ID, subscription ID if available, date of payment, and a short explanation of the issue.

5. Important Note

Bank reversals and card or UPI settlement timelines are handled partly by payment partners. Final credit timelines can vary depending on Razorpay, your bank, and the payment method used.
